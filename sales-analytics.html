BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE PROJECT

Sales & Customer Analytics Overview

An integrated business intelligence dashboard that combines sales performance indicators with customer analytics to provide a comprehensive view of business performance, revenue trends, and customer contribution.

Project Overview

This project provides a consolidated overview of sales and customer performance through interactive data visualizations. It helps management monitor key business indicators, evaluate sales trends, and understand the relationship between customer activity and revenue generation.

Business Objectives

  • Monitor overall sales performance.
  • Track customer growth and activity.
  • Understand revenue trends over time.
  • Evaluate customer contribution to sales.
  • Support management reporting and decisions.

Key Analysis Areas

  • Total sales value and sales quantity.
  • Monthly and daily sales trends.
  • Total number of customers.
  • Customer purchasing distribution.
  • Top-performing customers.
  • Sales performance comparisons.

Technical Approach

  • Data extraction and preparation.
  • Data transformation using Power Query.
  • Building a structured data model.
  • Developing DAX measures and KPIs.
  • Designing interactive dashboards.

Tools & Technologies

Power BI DAX SQL Server Power Query Data Modeling Data Analytics

Business Value

The dashboard provides management with a unified view of sales and customer performance, helping identify trends, evaluate business results, and make informed decisions based on reliable performance indicators.
