BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE PROJECT

Daily Sales Performance Dashboard

An interactive business intelligence dashboard designed to monitor daily sales performance, evaluate business performance indicators, and provide insights into sales trends and operational results.

Power BI SQL Server DAX Sales Analytics

Project Overview

This project demonstrates how business sales data can be transformed into an interactive reporting solution. The dashboard provides a structured view of sales performance, allowing business users to monitor key metrics and explore performance across different business dimensions.

Business Objectives

Sales Monitoring

Monitor sales performance and track business results over time.

Target Achievement

Evaluate sales performance against business targets and identify gaps.

Performance Comparison

Compare performance across branches, sales channels, and product categories.

Decision Support

Provide management with clear insights to support business decisions.

Key Analysis Areas

  • Daily and monthly sales performance.
  • Sales target achievement and performance gaps.
  • Branch and sales point performance.
  • Sales distribution across product categories.
  • Sales channel performance analysis.
  • Identification of sales trends and business patterns.

Technical Approach

Data Extraction

Using SQL Server to retrieve and organize business data for analytical reporting.

Data Modeling

Organizing data and defining relationships to support accurate analytical calculations.

Data Analysis

Applying DAX measures and analytical calculations to evaluate business KPIs.

Visualization

Designing interactive visual reports to communicate business performance clearly.

Tools & Technologies

Microsoft Power BI SQL Server DAX Data Modeling Business Intelligence Data Visualization

Business Value

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of sales performance, helping management identify trends, evaluate business results, compare operational performance, and make more informed decisions based on data.

Portfolio Demonstration: This dashboard uses fictional sample data and anonymized business information. It is presented for portfolio and demonstration purposes only.
