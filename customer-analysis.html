POWER BI PROJECT

Customer Purchase Analysis Dashboard

An interactive customer analytics dashboard designed to understand purchasing behavior, identify high-value customers, analyze revenue distribution, and support data-driven business decisions.

Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer purchasing patterns and understanding how customers contribute to overall business performance. The dashboard transforms sales transactions into meaningful insights that help management evaluate customer value and purchasing behavior.

Business Objectives

  • Identify the highest-value customers.
  • Understand customer purchasing behavior.
  • Analyze revenue contribution by customer.
  • Identify customer segments based on purchase value.
  • Monitor customer activity and purchasing trends.

Key Analysis Areas

  • Total sales and customer count.
  • Customer distribution by purchase value.
  • Top customers based on total purchases.
  • Average purchase value per customer.
  • Monthly customer purchasing trends.
  • Customer contribution to total revenue.

Technical Approach

  • Data extraction and preparation.
  • Data cleaning and transformation.
  • Data modeling and relationship management.
  • DAX measures for customer and sales KPIs.
  • Interactive visualizations and filtering.

Tools & Technologies

Power BI DAX SQL Server Power Query Data Modeling Data Visualization

Business Value

The dashboard helps decision-makers identify valuable customer segments, understand revenue concentration, recognize purchasing patterns, and support customer retention and sales strategies.

Portfolio Demo: This dashboard preview uses fictional sample data and is intended to demonstrate analytical concepts and visualization capabilities. It does not contain confidential company data.
